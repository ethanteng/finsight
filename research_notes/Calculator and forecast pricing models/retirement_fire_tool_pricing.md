# Retirement / FIRE / Coast FIRE Tool Pricing (US consumer market, as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-07. "Seen 2026-10-07" means the vendor's own page was fetched directly that day. Older or third-party prices carry their year. Aggregator and affiliate sites (Gerald/joingerald.com, AlternativeTo, SaaSworthy) contradicted each other and the vendor pages, so they are used only to flag conflicts. They are never the sole source for a price.

## Q1. What do competitors charge, and on what billing model?

### Takeaway
Paid DIY retirement planners cluster at **$80 to $289 per year, billed annually**: OnTrajectory $80, ProjectionLab $129, Pralana Online $129 first year then $99, Boldin $144 (rising to $168 on Oct 15, 2026), MaxiFi $149, WealthTrace $229 to $289. Most also have a free tier or trial. Monthly billing is rare: only OnTrajectory ($9/mo) and some iOS FIRE apps offer it, and ProjectionLab dropped it. Pure FIRE and Coast FIRE calculators (FIRECalc, cFIREsim, FI Calc, Engaging Data, WalletBurst) are free and ungated. They are funded by donations, affiliate ads or small one-time spreadsheet upsells. The big brokerages and publishers (Empower, Vanguard, Schwab, Fidelity, NerdWallet, SmartAsset) give calculators away to generate advisory or asset leads.

### Inferences
- The going rate for a full DIY retirement planner is roughly **$10 to $14 a month, billed annually** ($129 to $168/yr). Ask Linc's ~$5 one-week idea works out to about $260/yr if annualized. That is above every consumer planner's annual price, though a one-off $5 is far below any of them.
- Annual-only billing is now the norm for the leading planners (ProjectionLab, Boldin, Pralana, MaxiFi, WealthTrace). Monthly survives only at the low end (OnTrajectory, iOS apps).
- Nobody charges for a basic FIRE or Coast FIRE number. Paid value sits in tax and Roth modeling, Monte Carlo, saved and comparable scenarios, and exports.

### Gaps
- No direct read of Fidelity, Vanguard, Schwab, NerdWallet or Bankrate calculator pages. Their free status rests on third-party sources and general knowledge.
- Optimal Retirement Planner (i-orp.com) did not resolve on 2026-10-07, so its current status is unknown. It was historically free.

- Pralana Gold's current price and billing model are not published on its site.
- The prices of the FIRECalc supporter tier and of VeriPlan were not found.

## Q2. Which offer one-time / lifetime purchases vs subscriptions, and are there time-boxed passes?

### Takeaway
One-time pricing survives mainly in **spreadsheet and desktop tools**: TCRP $89.99 plus a $15.99 yearly upgrade, WalletBurst's $20 toolkit, Retirology at $19+ pay-what-you-want, Pralana Gold at $99 (2025), and a $9.99 iOS unlock. The best-known web lifetime deal, **ProjectionLab's $799 Lifetime**, was sold from June 2021 and no longer appears on its pricing page in 2026. **No time-boxed pass (7-day, 30-day, 1-month access) was found anywhere in the category.** The closest analogues are free trials (7 to 14 days), ProjectionLab keeping access through the end of a cancelled billing period, and introductory teaser pricing.

### Cited Findings

- ProjectionLab sunset self-hosting on March 6, 2025: "We've decided to focus entirely on the web version". 99.4% of users were already on the web app, existing installs keep working, and bug-fix support ended Dec 31, 2025 — [ProjectionLab blog](https://projectionlab.com/blog/update-on-self-hosting); [changelog](https://projectionlab.com/changelog)

### Inferences
- A **$5 one-week pass would be novel** in this category, with no direct comparable to benchmark conversion against. The nearest analogues are $9.99 to $20 one-time unlocks and spreadsheets.
- ProjectionLab took about four years to retire both self-hosting and the lifetime SKU, while its MRR grew roughly 8x from $10K to $83K. That suggests the market leader decided recurring revenue beats lifetime once demand is proven. This is an inference: no explicit announcement that Lifetime was discontinued was found.
- Pay-what-you-want and donations exist (FI Calc, FIRECalc supporters, Retirology), but only at hobbyist or very early scale. No evidence was found that they produce meaningful revenue.

### Gaps
- No public announcement of when or why ProjectionLab stopped selling Lifetime. The evidence only brackets it between Aug 2025 (WCI) and Jul 2026 (Rob Berger).
- Not confirmed whether existing ProjectionLab Lifetime holders keep web access.
- **No examples of credit/points-based pricing** were found in the retirement or FIRE category.
- No conversion data comparing trial lengths, or lifetime vs annual take-rates, at any vendor.

## Q3. What is free vs paywalled, and is the FIRE / Coast FIRE calculator category overwhelmingly free?

### Takeaway
Yes. Every dedicated FIRE and Coast FIRE calculator checked is free and shows results **without an email gate** (FIRECalc, cFIREsim, FI Calc, Engaging Data, WalletBurst). Even the paid planners now give away the headline math: ProjectionLab's free tier includes Monte Carlo and historical backtesting, and its tax estimation became free in Oct 2025. The paywall sits on **persistence (saving plans), tax and Roth optimization, multiple scenarios, withdrawal sequencing and exports**. Boldin and MaxiFi are the exceptions, keeping Monte Carlo behind the paywall.

### Cited Findings
- ProjectionLab Basic (free) includes "Forecasting, Financial Planning, Monte Carlo Simulations, Historical Backtesting, Flexible Modeling". Premium adds cash-flow projections, tax optimization, withdrawal strategies, Roth conversions, ACA subsidies, downloadable reports, multiple plans and compare mode — [ProjectionLab pricing](https://projectionlab.com/pricing)
- ProjectionLab Basic: "Data is not saved between sessions" — [Rob Berger, Jul 2026](https://robberger.com/projectionlab-review/). Making Sense of Cents: "if you want to save and revisit your plans later ... you'll need the Premium plan" — [Making Sense of Cents, Jul 2025](https://www.makingsenseofcents.com/2025/07/projectionlab-review.html)
- ProjectionLab v4.4.0 (Oct 13, 2025): "Tax estimation is now free"; detailed tax insights stay Premium — [changelog](https://projectionlab.com/changelog)
- Boldin's free Basic tier covers plan building, "what if" scenarios, 2 explorers, 10+ charts and limited AI. **Monte Carlo**, tax insights, withdrawal strategy, the Roth explorer and detailed budgeting are paid — [Boldin pricing](http://www.boldin.com/retirement/pricing/)
- Boldin's founder in 2021: "anyone should be able to build and manage a baseline plan for free, forever" — [Boldin founder note, Feb 27 2021](https://www.boldin.com/retirement/note-from-the-founder-newretirement-pricing-update/)
- MaxiFi's trial includes a lifetime plan, a "safe spending number" and up to 3 what-if profiles. The Roth optimizer, Social Security maximizer, Monte Carlo, PDF/Excel exports and 25 profiles need the paid plan — [MaxiFi pricing](https://www.maxifi.com/pricing)
- OnTrajectory's free Basic includes a "Chance of Success" calculation and 1 alternate scenario. Monte Carlo and historical analysis, unlimited scenarios and tax customization are paid — [OnTrajectory](https://www.ontrajectory.com/)
- WalletBurst shows its Coast FIRE calculator free with no gate. The newsletter signup is optional ("Sign up to be notified when I launch new tools"), and the paid upsell is an "advanced" $20 spreadsheet — [WalletBurst](https://walletburst.com/tools/coast-fire-calc/)
- Engaging Data's FIRE calculator needs no signup, and inputs are not transmitted to its server — [Engaging Data](https://engaging-data.com/fire-calculator/)
- FIRECalc: no signup; "does not store any information you enter"; login exists only for supporter features — [FIRECalc](https://www.firecalc.com/)
- FI Calc: free, donation-supported, with CSV export and share links — [FI Calc](https://ficalc.app/)
- Free Coast FIRE mobile apps include Finance FIRE ("no subscriptions or hidden fees") and My Coast Fire Calculator. Clark.com and Richify publish free web Coast FIRE calculators — [Finance FIRE, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kh/app/finance-fire/id6746294826); [Clark.com](https://clark.com/coast-fire-calculator/); [Richify](https://www.richify.ai/us/guides/how-to-calculate-coast-fire-number)
- Empower describes its planner as "100% Free". Using it involves a signup form that opts the user into marketing offers and calls — [Empower](https://www.empower.com/retire)

### Inferences
- Ask Linc's approach of gating a basic Coast FIRE or retirement result behind email plus signup is **stricter than every free Coast FIRE competitor checked**. All of them show the number immediately. Charging $5 for that same basic number would compete with many free, ungated substitutes one search away.
- ProjectionLab's choice to gate **saving and revisiting**, not the result itself, is the closest precedent to Ask Linc's "the answer opens in Ask Linc" handoff. The free user sees value, and continuity is what's paid. Ask Linc's leverage is likewise the personalized, linked-data follow-up, not the calculator number.
- The free-vs-paid line has shifted toward giving more away. ProjectionLab made tax estimation free in 2025, and Monte Carlo is free at ProjectionLab and OnTrajectory's "Chance of Success". This is consistent with free tiers acting as acquisition funnels.

### Gaps
- No data on how many visitors abandon an email-gated calculator versus an ungated one in this category.
- Engaging Data's and cFIREsim's monetization (if any) was not confirmed beyond what their pages show.

## Q4. Public evidence on results: revenue, pricing changes, and sentiment

### Takeaway
The strongest public evidence comes from **ProjectionLab**: bootstrapped to **$1M ARR (June 2025)**, with **non-recurring revenue (Lifetime plans plus 1:1 training) adding 20 to 50% on top of MRR**, more than 100k households, and growth driven by HN, FIRE podcasts and influencer mentions rather than paid ads. **Boldin** has raised PlannerPlus repeatedly while grandfathering existing subscribers: $4/mo (2018–19), then $6/mo, then $96/yr (2021), $120/yr, $144/yr (Jul 2025) and $168/yr (Oct 2026). Forum sentiment shows a real segment that dislikes rising annual fees and will pay for lifetime, alongside complaints that $800 lifetime is steep. Reddit itself could not be accessed.

## Q5. How do free lead-gen calculators (NerdWallet, SmartAsset, Empower) monetize, and what is a lead worth?

### Takeaway
Free calculators at big publishers and asset managers are **advisor-lead or AUM funnels**. Disclosed lead values: Empower pays referring sites **up to $250 per lead**. NerdWallet earns **$13 to $1,150 per referral** depending on the user's investable assets, plus conversion fees ($500 or a revenue share), and gets **100% of first-year advisory fees** from its affiliated RIA. SmartAsset historically charged **$90 to $190 per lead** by assets and now sells subscription bundles of referrals. Critically, **users with under $100K investable assets are not monetized** in NerdWallet's advisor flow. Lead value scales with assets, so younger Coast FIRE audiences are worth little as advisor leads.

### Cited Findings
**NerdWallet Advisors** (disclosure effective Sep 2, 2025) — [NerdWallet disclosure PDF](https://cdns3.nerdwallet.com/nerdwallet-advisors/disclosures/promoter_solicitor_20250902.pdf)

| Arrangement | What NerdWallet Advisors receives |
|---|---|
| Fixed fee per referral, paid whether or not the user becomes a client | $13 to $1,150, depending on the user's reported investable assets and exclusivity |
| Conversion-contingent fee | $500 one-time, or a percentage of the advisor's revenue |
| Non-exclusive referral-platform lead (third-party referral platform) | $100 to $300 per qualified lead, plus a revenue share starting at 100% and falling to 25% over three years if the user converts |
| Exclusive referral-platform lead | A revenue share starting at 100% and falling to 74% over ten years |
| NerdWallet Wealth Partners (affiliated RIA) | 100% of first-year advisory fees for referred clients |
| NerdWallet Coach (Uprise), users with $100K to $500K | $13 to $300 per referral |
| Users with under $100K investable assets | Referrals are uncompensated |

The disclosure also says users are matched with up to three advisors, and "the selection of advisors that are displayed is influenced by the compensation NWA receives".

**Empower**
- The planner page discloses that a referring site is paid "**up to $250 for each individual lead**", and advisory services are "provided for a fee by Empower Advisory Group" based on assets managed — [empower.com/retire](https://www.empower.com/retire) (seen 2026-10-07)
- Its wealth management fee is reported at 0.89% up to $1M (e.g. $890/yr on $100K). This is third-party reporting — [CreditDonkey](https://www.creditdonkey.com/personal-capital.html)
- A competitor's marketing page reports that users with six-figure portfolios get calls from Empower advisors, "sometimes persistently". This is a competitor claim and anecdotal — [FinancialAha](https://www.financialaha.com/zh/alternatives/empower/)

**SmartAsset**
- 2018: leads cost **$190** for investors with $1M+ and **$90** for $250K to $1M — [WealthManagement](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/financial-technology/focus-financial-leads-28-million-funding-round-for-lead-generation-platform)
- July 2021: leads cost $90 to $190 by assets, with a **5 to 20% conversion rate**, and may be sent to multiple advisers — [InvestmentNews, Jul 2021](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/the-latest-in-financial-advisertech-july-2021/208962). Enrollment is free and advisors set their monthly lead volume; validated investors are matched with up to three advisors — [National Life ESI blog](https://esiblog.nationallife.com/?p=2071)
- Industry context (2021): matching services charge "anywhere from a $1,000 flat fee per month to less than $100 per lead". Ongoing revenue-share models run 15 to 25% of revenue for the client's lifetime — [Kitces AdvisorTech, Jul 2021](https://www.kitces.com/blog/the-latest-in-financial-advisortech-july-2021/)
- SmartAsset raised $110M in 2021 with revenues of about $100M (RIABiz headline) and reached a ~$1B valuation driven by the advisor-referral business — [RIABiz, Jul 2021](https://riabiz.com/a/2021/7/13/smartasset-raises-110-million-after-lpl-and-schwab-dominoes-fall-revenues-zoom-to-100-million-and-zoe-escalates-ria-referrals-arms-race); [Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2auctyvi71ne1npxu7z7k/ria-intel/advisor-referral-business-helps-lift-smartasset-to-1-billion-valuation)
- SmartAsset's Advisor Marketing Platform (AMP) launched March 2024. It delivered **89,000+ referrals** by March 2025, with an estimated average AUM of **$986K per lead**. Subscriptions buy a fixed range of referrals over 6 or 12 months — [Bradford Era syndication of SmartAsset release, Mar 26 2025](https://bradfordera.com/2025/03/26/smartassets-advisor-marketing-platform-amp-surpasses-89000-high-value-referrals-delivered-to-financial-advisors)
- SmartAsset's disclosures: referral fees are "determined between each Advisor and SmartAsset". Some are cost-per-lead, some a portion of ongoing advisor fees, some subscription-based — [SmartAsset disclosures](https://smartasset.com/important-disclosures)
- A 2024 RIABiz headline reported RIAs being pushed toward about $25K/yr arrangements. Only the headline was seen — [RIABiz SmartAsset tag](https://riabiz.com/tags/smartasset)

**Brokerage calculators (Vanguard, Schwab, Fidelity)**
- These are free. The WCI site notes basic calculators like Schwab's often lead toward paid advisory services — [WCI](https://www.whitecoatinvestor.com/best-retirement-calculators/)

**Affiliate monetization on small FIRE sites**
- WalletBurst runs ProjectionLab and Nectarine ads with referral parameters next to its free Coast FIRE calculator — [WalletBurst](https://walletburst.com/tools/coast-fire-calc/)
- FIRECalc carries Amazon affiliate links — [FIRECalc](https://www.firecalc.com/)

### Inferences
- Advisor-lead value is driven almost entirely by **investable assets**. NerdWallet pays nothing for users under $100K, and SmartAsset's top tier starts at $1M. A Coast FIRE audience, typically younger and earlier in accumulation, likely sits mostly below the monetizable threshold. Monetizing Ask Linc's calculator traffic as advisor leads would likely be worth little per visitor unless it skews to older, high-asset retirement-calculator users.
- At $13 to $250 per lead for mid-asset users, an advisor lead can be worth more than a $5 one-off charge, but only for the fraction of users with meaningful assets. It would also conflict with Ask Linc's positioning as a neutral assistant. All three lead-gen players disclose material conflicts of interest from this model.
- Large players treat calculators as cost centers feeding AUM: Empower at roughly 0.89%/yr, and NerdWallet capturing 100% of first-year fees through its own RIA. Neither directly charges for calculation, which reinforces the market expectation that calculators are free.
